[size=5][b]Overview[/b][/size]
The [b]Arcane Invoker[/b] is a Wizard subclass that ports Invoker's signature kit into Baldur's Gate 3. You conjure up to three [b]reagent orbs[/b] — [b]Quas[/b], [b]Wex[/b] and [b]Exort[/b] — then cast [b]Invoke[/b] to unleash the one spell that matches the orbs you're holding. Orbs are free to conjure; the combo is your turn. Ten combos, each true to its Dota role, built almost entirely from remixed base-game effects — so there are [b]no asset dependencies[/b].

[size=5][b]The Three Orbs[/b][/size]
Just holding an orb grants a passive, stacking up to three:
[list]
[*][b][color=#5cc6ff]Quas[/color][/b] (Ice) — +1 HP regenerated per turn in combat, per Quas held.
[*][b][color=#b98bff]Wex[/color][/b] (Storm) — movement costs 10% less and +1 Initiative, per Wex held.
[*][b][color=#ff8f43]Exort[/color][/b] (Fire) — +1 force damage to your attacks & Arcane Strike, per Exort held.
[/list]

[size=5][b]How It Works[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*][b]Conjure[/b] — cast Quas, Wex or Exort to hold up to 3 orbs. No action cost — build freely.
[*][b]Invoke[/b] — open Invoke and the combo matching your held orbs lights up; the other nine stay greyed out. One click to fire.
[*][b]Reset[/b] — orbs persist after invoking; cast [b]Dispel Orbs[/b] (free) to clear them and mix a new recipe.
[/list]

[size=5][b]The Ten Combos[/b][/size]
[i]Order doesn't matter — only which three orbs you hold. Damage shown from level 1 → level 12 (the base-game cap); it climbs as you level. Levels 13–20 are covered below.[/i]
[list]
[*][b]Cold Snap[/b] (QQQ) — [i]At-will.[/i] A 3-turn frost DoT (1d4+1 → 3d4+3 per tick) that snaps the target with a 1-turn freeze.
[*][b]Ghost Walk[/b] (QQW) — [i]At-will.[/i] Turn invisible with a haunting aura — nearby foes are slowed and burned while you move faster and can't be held.
[*][b]Ice Wall[/b] (QQE) — [i]At-will.[/i] Drag out a wall of ice. It can be walked through, but foes within 2m of it are slowed and burned (1d6 → 3d6 fire per turn).
[*][b]EMP[/b] (WWW) — [i]Lv2 slot.[/i] A crackling ball of energy charges over the targeted area, then detonates in a storm burst (2d8 → 7d8) that Silences everything caught (two turns on a failed save, one on a success).
[*][b]Tornado[/b] (QWW) — [i]Lv2 slot.[/i] A lingering cyclone: knocks enemies prone as they enter, then slows and batters them (1d4+4 → 3d6+6 force per turn).
[*][b]Deafening Blast[/b] (QWE) — [i]At-will.[/i] A thunder cone (2d8 → 5d8) that knocks foes back and leaves them disarmed (attacks at disadvantage).
[*][b]Alacrity[/b] (WWE) — [i]Lv2 slot.[/i] One buff for 3 turns: Haste (no exhaustion afterward), +1d4 to attack rolls and +1d6 damage on every hit.
[*][b]Sun Strike[/b] (EEE) — [i]Lv3 slot.[/i] Mark any spot you can see with a golden ring and a needle of sunlight; a second later a column of solar fire crashes down on it (4d10 → 12d10) with a small blast radius. No line of sight from you needed, and no saving throw. With Script Extender its damage is rolled once and split evenly between every creature in the blast, so a lone target takes all of it. Without it, creatures in the centre take full damage and enemies in a wider ring take a smaller burn.
[*][b]Chaos Meteor[/b] (WEE) — [i]Lv3 slot.[/i] A flaming boulder falls from the sky and shatters where it lands (3d8 → 8d8 thunder), setting everything it hits ablaze for 3 turns (1d6 → 3d6 fire per turn) and leaves a burning fire field behind.
[*][b]Forge Spirit[/b] (QEE) — [i]Lv2 slot.[/i] Summon two fire elemental spirits for 4 turns. They throw Fire Bolts and burn foes in melee, and grow tougher and hit harder as you level.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Beyond the Combos[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Invoker's tempo[/b] — two actions per turn from level 2.
[*][b]Scales with you[/b] — combo damage climbs every couple of levels (Sun Strike and Chaos Meteor more than double by level 11), from the Nautiloid to the endgame, and on to level 20 if you use a level-cap mod (see below).
[*][b]Bonus spell slots[/b] — choosing the subclass grants one extra 3rd-level and two extra 2nd-level spell slots, so the heavy hitters are castable from level 2. They refresh on a long rest.
[*][b]Arcane Gauntlets[/b] — signature gloves granted at the start: an at-will [b]Arcane Strike[/b] force bolt and an Intelligence boost.
[*][b]Reagent economy[/b] — free orbs, at-will utility combos, and spell-slot-gated heavy hitters keep it strong but fair.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Compatible with Expansion (Levels 13–20)[/b][/size]
Arcane Invoker works with [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/279]Expansion: Level 13-20 (Configurable)[/url]. It is [b]not required[/b] — the base game stops at level 12 and everything above works without it — but if you raise the level cap, the Arcane Invoker keeps growing:
[list]
[*][b]Damage to level 20[/b] — at levels 19–20: Sun Strike 16d10, Chaos Meteor impact 12d8, EMP 11d8, Deafening Blast 7d8. Cold Snap, Ghost Walk, Ice Wall, Tornado, the Meteor burn and the Forge Spirits gain two more tiers (levels 13 and 17).
[*][b]Level 14 — Elemental Attunement:[/b] resistance to Cold, Fire and Lightning damage.
[*][b]Level 16 — Invoker's Reserves:[/b] one extra 3rd-level and one extra 2nd-level spell slot.
[*][b]Level 18 — Triple Resonance:[/b] while you hold 3 orbs, +2 AC and +2 to all saving throws.
[*][b]Level 20 — Grand Invoker:[/b] Intelligence +2 (maximum 22) and +5 Initiative.
[/list]
[b]Load order:[/b] Mod Configuration Menu → Expansion → InvokerAbilities. [b]Tested through level 12;[/b] the level 13–20 additions have not been through a full playthrough yet, so please report any problems.

[size=5][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Baldur's Gate 3[/b] — current patch.
[*][b]BG3 Mod Manager[/b] — to install and order the mod.
[*][b]BG3 Script Extender[/b] — Optional. Auto-grants the Arcane Gauntlets and lets Sun Strike split its damage between everything it hits. The subclass, orbs and all ten combos work without it.
[*][b]Expansion: Level 13-20[/b] — Optional. Only needed to level past 12 (it requires Script Extender and Mod Configuration Menu itself).
[/list]

[size=5][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Add [b]InvokerAbilities.pak[/b] via BG3 Mod Manager (or drop it into your Mods folder).
[*]Add it to your load order and export to the game. If you also use Expansion, load Mod Configuration Menu first, then Expansion, then InvokerAbilities.
[*]Launch. Create a Wizard, reach [b]level 2[/b], and choose [b]Arcane Invoker[/b]. (Already past level 2? Respec at Withers and pick Wizard, then choose the subclass at level 2.)
[/list]
