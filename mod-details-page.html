Overview

One caster, ten spells, three reagent orbs

The Arcane Invoker is a Wizard subclass that ports Invoker's signature kit into Baldur's Gate 3. You conjure up to three reagent orbs — Quas, Wex, and Exort — then cast Invoke to unleash the one spell that matches the orbs you're holding. Orbs are free to conjure; the combo is your turn. Ten combos, each true to its Dota role, built almost entirely from remixed base-game effects so there are no asset dependencies.

The Three Orbs

Holding an orb is its own rewardEach orb you keep in hand grants a passive, stacking up to three — just like Dota.

Quas

Ice · Vitality

+1 HP regenerated per turn during combat, per Quas held.

Wex

Storm · Agility

Movement costs 10% less and +1 Initiative, per Wex held.

Exort

Fire · Power

+1 force damage to your attacks & Arcane Strike, per Exort held.

The Loop

Conjure, invoke, repeat

I.

Conjure

Cast Quas, Wex, or Exort to hold up to three orbs. It costs no action — build freely on your turn.

II.

Invoke

Open Invoke and the combo matching your held orbs lights up — the other nine stay greyed out. One click to fire.

III.

Reset

Orbs persist after invoking; cast Dispel Orbs (free) to clear them and mix a new recipe.

The Arsenal

Ten combosOrder doesn't matter — only which three orbs you hold. Damage shown as level 1 → top tier; it climbs as you level.

At-will

Cold Snap QQQ

A 3-turn frost DoT (1d4+1 → 3d4+3 per tick) that snaps the target with a 1-turn freeze.

At-will

Ghost Walk QQW

Turn invisible with a haunting aura — nearby foes are slowed and burned (1d6 → 3d6 per turn) while you move faster and can't be held.

At-will

Ice Wall QQE

Drag out a wall of ice. It can be walked through, but foes within 2 m of it are slowed and burned (1d6 → 3d6 fire per turn).

Lv2 slot

EMP WWW

A crackling ball of energy charges over any targeted area, then detonates in a storm burst (2d8 → 7d8) that silences everything caught (two turns on a failed save, one on a success).

Lv2 slot

Tornado QWW

A lingering cyclone: knocks enemies prone as they enter, then slows and batters them (1d4+4 → 3d6+6 force per turn).

At-will

Deafening Blast QWE

A thunder cone (2d8 → 5d8) that knocks foes back and leaves them disarmed — attacks at disadvantage.

Lv2 slot

Alacrity WWE

One buff for 3 turns: Haste (no exhaustion afterward), +1d4 to attack rolls and +1d6 damage on every hit.

Lv3 slot

Sun Strike EEE

Mark any spot you can see with a golden ring and a needle of sunlight; a second later a column of solar fire crashes down on it (3d10 → 8d10). No line of sight from you needed. With Script Extender its damage is rolled once and split evenly between every creature in the blast, so a lone target takes all of it. Without it, creatures in the centre take full damage and enemies in a wider ring take a smaller burn.

Lv3 slot

Chaos Meteor WEE

A flaming boulder falls from the sky and shatters where it lands (3d8 → 8d8 thunder), setting everything it hits ablaze for 3 turns (1d6 → 3d6 fire per turn) and leaving a burning fire field behind.

Lv2 slot

Forge Spirit QEE

Summon two fire elemental spirits for 4 turns. They throw Fire Bolts and burn foes in melee, and grow tougher and hit harder as you level.

Beyond the Combos

The full kit

Invoker's tempo

Two actions per turn from level 2 — build orbs and still fire a combo, or chain two spells.

Scales with you

Combo damage climbs every couple of levels (Sun Strike and Chaos Meteor more than double by level 11), so the kit keeps pace from the Nautiloid to the endgame.

Arcane Gauntlets

Signature gloves granted at the start: an at-will Arcane Strike bolt of light and an Intelligence boost.

Reagent economy

Free orbs, at-will utility combos, and spell-slot-gated heavy hitters keep it strong but fair.

Requirements

Before you begin

  • Baldur's Gate 3 — current patch.
  • BG3 Mod Manager — to install and order the mod.
  • Script Extender optional
    Optional. Auto-grants the Arcane Gauntlets and lets Sun Strike split its damage between everything it hits. The subclass, orbs, and all ten combos work without it.

Installation

Three steps

  1. Drop InvokerAbilities.pak into your BG3 Mods folder (or import it in BG3 Mod Manager).
  2. Add it to your load order and export to the game.
  3. Launch. Create a Wizard, reach level 2, and choose Arcane Invoker as your subclass.
